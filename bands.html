Together, or not

Nobody has to be free at the same time.

When two of you are in a song at once you can see it and talk in it. When you are not, the song is still moving — somebody adds a verse on a train, somebody else puts a lead on it at midnight, and it is all there in the morning.

Gigs

Setlists that leave the app.

Build a set from the songs you have, reorder it, and print it or send it as a PDF. The people standing in for the night do not need an account to read it.

Credit

Who brought what, on the record.

Lines carry the colour of whoever wrote them and takes carry the name of whoever played them, from the moment they land. Not because anybody is keeping score, but because a year later nobody remembers, and the conversation about splits goes better when the answer is already written down.
