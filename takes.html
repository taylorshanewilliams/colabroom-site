Nobody gets overwritten

Mute anything. It only goes quiet for you.

Wondering how it sounds without the harmony is a normal thing to wonder, and asking should not cost anybody their part. Muting is yours alone — the person who played it never hears a difference.

  • Your takes are yours to balance. Levels and timing stay where the player put them, and only they can move them.
  • Only the player can delete. Or a Room's owner. Everyone else mutes, which is what they wanted anyway.
  • Nothing is a conflict. Two people recording at once both land — takes add up rather than fight.
Sideways

Turn the phone. It becomes a desk.

A list is right for reading and wrong for balancing. Sideways, the takes sit next to each other as faders — because deciding whether the harmony sits well against the lead means comparing them, not scrolling between them.
