Why it is free

Because it is the cheap half.

Finding chords is a few seconds of ordinary computing. Splitting a song into its instruments is not — that is the part that costs, and that is the part the app does. This page runs the first half and nothing else, which is why it can be free and why the key above is an estimate rather than a measurement.

Your recording

We do not keep it.

The audio is read, listened to, and dropped. There is no account, no upload folder, and no copy on a server afterwards — which is not a promise about our conduct so much as a description of how this is built. Nothing was written down, so there is nothing to delete.
