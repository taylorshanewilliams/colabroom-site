The Control Room

It listens back and writes it down.

A real studio has a live room and a control room, and so does this. When the playing is done, one button makes the song sheet: the key, the tempo, the chords in bars, where the chorus starts, and the words you sang — off one pass of a phone recording.

Stuck on a chord

Tap it. It shows you how to play it.

Every chord on the sheet holds an answer. Tap one for the shape, the notes in it, and the five notes that work over it. Tap the key for the scale, the chords that belong to it, and where to put a capo — worked out in your song's key, not in somebody's example key.

Your account, your songs, and deleting them

Catalogs are private to their members. Voice notes, takes and reference recordings are stored with the catalog they belong to. Imported documents are read on your own device.

To delete your account

  1. Open CoLabRoom and go to the Account screen.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This is immediate and permanent, with no undo and no recovery window. It removes your profile, your lyric lines, comments and invitations — and every catalog you own, including its songs and everything your bandmates added to them. There is currently no way to hand a catalog to someone else first, so if you own one your band is using, make sure everyone has their own copy of anything they would want to keep before you delete your account.

If you cannot sign in and need your account removed, email support@colabroom.com from the address you signed up with.

How long takes are kept

Takes nobody has opened for 90 days are removed, so storage does not grow forever. You are warned before it happens, opening a song resets the clock, and everything can be exported from inside the app at any time.

It is early

This is a beta built by one person. Things will be rough in places. Feedback lives inside every song and comes straight to me rather than to a ticket queue.
