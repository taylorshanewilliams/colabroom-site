Your account, your songs, and deleting them

Rooms are private to their members. Voice notes, takes and reference recordings are stored with the Room they belong to. Imported documents are read on your own device.

To delete your account

  1. Open CoLabRoom and go to the Account tab.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This is immediate and permanent, with no undo and no recovery window. It removes your profile, your lyric lines, comments and invitations — and every Room you own, including its song projects and everything your bandmates added to them. There is currently no way to hand a Room to someone else first, so if you own one your band is using, make sure everyone has their own copy of anything they would want to keep before you delete your account.

If you cannot sign in and need your account removed, email support@colabroom.com from the address you signed up with.

How long takes are kept

Takes nobody has opened for 90 days are removed, so storage does not grow forever. You are warned before it happens, opening a song resets the clock, and everything can be exported from inside the app at any time.

It is early

This is a beta built by one person. Things will be rough in places. Feedback lives inside every song and comes straight to me rather than to a ticket queue.
