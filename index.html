[ Intro ]

The app should be the easy part.

Nobody picked up a guitar to learn software. CoLabRoom stays out of the way — write, play, and walk away knowing more about the song than when you started.

Nothing to set up

Make a Room, make a song, start typing. No templates, no fields, no configuration.

Nothing to file

It saves as you write, and names your recordings after what you sang in them.

Something to show for it

One take gives you the key, the tempo, the chords and the shape of the song.

Nothing hiding in a menu

Analyze, Record and Live sit right on the screen — no digging.

[ Verse ] — Writing

A page, not a form.

One flowing page for the whole song. Type, paste, delete a verse, keep writing — no boxes, no fields, nothing to fill out first.

Everyone has a colour

A coloured dot beside every line shows who wrote it — attribution without a comment thread.

Voice notes on the line itself

Hum the melody where the words are. Tap the dot, sing the part, and it lives on that line.

It saves while you write

No save button. Edits reach the room as they happen, and the page says so plainly if one doesn't.

Headers it recognises

Type [Chorus] and it looks like one. No formatting menu.

[ Chorus ] — Analysis

Play it once. Find out what you played.

Record a take, or upload a demo you already have. You get back the things you would otherwise work out by ear at two in the morning.

  • KeyThe key it is actually in, not the one you meant to play.
  • TempoBPM, and the beat positions everything else aligns to.
  • ChordsA beat-aligned chord map, with how much of the song it is sure about.
  • StructureWhere the verses, choruses and instrumentals fall — renameable to whatever the band calls them.
  • InstrumentsWhat it heard playing.
  • LyricsA transcript timed to the audio, word by word.
  • StemsThe take split into parts, so you can play along with the drums and leave your own guitar out.

[ Verse ] — The Studio

You can't skim audio.

That's why musicians end up with eighty voice memos called Recording 8/23 11:40 and never play a single one back.

So the Studio names an idea after the first thing you actually sing in it, and only ever replaces a placeholder — never a title you typed yourself.

Then it makes them searchable by their words. Half a remembered line is enough to find the recording, and the result tells you which line matched.

[ Bridge ] — Song Sheet

Chords where the chords go.

Above the words, at the syllable they land on. Move one, add one, delete one. Rotate the phone for a two-column sheet you can read from a music stand, then print it or send the band a PDF.

[ Chorus ] — Live

For the part where you actually play it.

Live mode scrolls the song so your hands stay on the instrument. With an analysed recording it follows the real per-line timing — waiting through the intro, holding through the instrumental — instead of creeping down the page at a constant speed.

Or just set a speed

No recording yet? Slow, medium, fast, or spread across the song's length. It works before the song is finished.

Count-in and Tap Sync

A count-in before it moves, and one tap to put it back where you are when the band takes the long way round.

[ Verse ] — Import

Bring the songs you already have.

Your back catalogue is in a notes app, three PDFs and a spreadsheet. CoLabRoom reads PDF, Word, RTF, Excel, CSV, OpenDocument, Markdown, plain text, a public Google Sheet link, or anything you paste — and shows you the lyric lines, section labels and chords it found before a single one lands.

[ Verse ] — The band

One place, every writer.

A Room holds a band's songs, visible only to its members. Every song keeps its own record of what changed and who said what — right where you're already writing.

Setlists

Build a set from songs across Rooms, reorder it, and print or share the whole thing at once.

A Toolbox that stays put

Open chord shapes, capo chart and scale formulas for guitar; tuning, scale formulas and root-note patterns for bass. Offline.

Your account and your songs

Rooms are private to their members. Voice notes and reference recordings are stored with the Room they belong to. Imported documents are read on your own device.

To delete your account:

  1. Open CoLabRoom and go to the Account tab.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This is immediate and permanent, with no undo and no recovery window. It removes your profile, your lyric lines, comments and invitations — and every Room you own, including its song projects and everything your bandmates added to them. There is currently no way to hand a Room to someone else first, so if you own one your band is using, make sure everyone has their own copy of anything they'd want to keep before you delete your account.

If you cannot sign in and need your account removed, email support@colabroom.com from the address you signed up with.
