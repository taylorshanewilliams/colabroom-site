Honesty

It tells you when it is guessing.

Chord detection is good, not perfect, and a machine cannot know that the repeated bit is the chorus rather than the verse. So every answer carries how much of the song it actually covered, anything uncertain is flagged rather than smoothed over, and you can correct any of it in a tap. A confident wrong answer is worse than an honest gap.
