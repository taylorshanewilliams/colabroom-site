What moves

    
The chords. Nothing else.

    

      Your words stay where you put them, your tab stays tab, your notes to the
      drummer stay your notes to the drummer. The only other things that change
      are the key line and the capo line at the top, because if those said the
      old key the chart would be lying.
    

  


  

    
Capo

    
A capo moves your hand, not the song.

    

      Put one on the 4th fret, play G shapes, and the band still hears B. So the
      line above your chart names both — the shapes in front of you, and the key
      you have to say out loud to everybody else. Take the capo off and the
      chords go back up by the same four frets.
    

  


  

    
The fiddly bit

    
G/B up two is A/C♯.

    

      Not A/B. The note under the bass player's hand moves too, and a lot of
      transposers forget. Sharps and flats follow the key you land in, as well:
      up one from D is E♭ major, so D/A comes back E♭/B♭ rather than D♯/A♯.
      Anything you spelled yourself is left exactly as you spelled it.
